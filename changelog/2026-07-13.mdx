## Highlights

- **Account settings in the app**. Open the menu to manage your password, email addresses, and active sessions without leaving ProductBrain.

## Other improvements

- **Invites work when you already have an account**. If you open an invite while signed in under a different account, ProductBrain now asks which identity to use and takes you into the shared projects as the invited guest, instead of quietly leaving you in your current account.
- **Archived projects stay out of the switcher**. Archiving a project now also removes it from the project switcher, not just the main list.
- **The API-key menu stays on Free past the trial window**. Free accounts keep standing API access, and the menu to manage the key no longer disappears once the old trial period ends.
- **Clearer mutation-API errors**. A rejected change now names exactly what was wrong, so your agent can fix it without guessing.
